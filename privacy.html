Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026.

yt-factory is a private tool operated by a single person. It connects to the YouTube Data API to upload videos to one channel owned by the operator.

What is processed

  • An OAuth refresh token for the operator's own Google account, stored locally and never shared.
  • Public video metadata (titles, view counts) read from the YouTube Data API for research.
  • Video files produced by the operator and uploaded to the operator's channel.

What is not collected

No data from viewers or third parties. No analytics, no cookies, no tracking, no advertising. This website is static and does not set cookies.

Third parties

Google (YouTube API) processes the uploads. Google's privacy policy applies to that processing: policies.google.com/privacy.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The token is then invalid immediately.
